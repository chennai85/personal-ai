## Always check (any document with money)
- Total in the document's currency **and** in my home currency. Code does the math, not the model.
- Per person / per couple / per night, when the document is for a group.
- What is **not** included. List it separately.
- Quantities against headcount: if 8 people travel, every per-person line should say 8.
- Lines that contradict each other (dates, durations, room types, counts).
- **Non-refundable** money: how much, and when it becomes non-refundable.
- Deadlines: deposits, final payments, cancellation windows. Give the calendar date.
- "Prices subject to change" and anything else that can move after I say yes.
- Currency risk: what a 5% move costs me.
- Bank details in a document: remind me to verify by phone before any wire.

## Travel documents, also check
- Entry papers needed for my passport.
- Daily timing: arrival time vs. connection vs. check-in.
- Meals: which nights are included and which are not.
- Physical demands: walking-only golf, long drives, early starts.
- Weather-dependent activities and the backup plan.

## Never
- Never decide for me. Show the choice and what it depends on.
- Never send, pay, sign, book or delete anything.
- Never copy account numbers, card numbers or passwords into a brief.
- Never fill in a blank vault field with a guess. Say "not in my vault".

## Always end with
- What only I can decide.
- What the vault didn't know (blank fields that would have helped).
